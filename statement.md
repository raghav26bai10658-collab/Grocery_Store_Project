## Problem Statement
Small grocery stores often rely on manual, paper-based tracking for inventory and billing, leading to calculation errors, misplaced records, and unawareness of low stock levels.

## Scope of the Project
This project provides a digital Command Line Interface (CLI) solution to digitize inventory tracking, automate checkout math, and alert owners of low stock levels. It uses local CSV storage to ensure data persists without needing a complex cloud database.

## Target Users
* Store Owners/Admins (Managing inventory and reports)
* Cashiers (Processing daily sales)

## High-Level Features
* Persistent Data Storage (CSV)
* CRUD Operations for Inventory
* Cart and Billing Engine
* Automated Low Stock Reporting
